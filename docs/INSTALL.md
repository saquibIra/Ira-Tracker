# Getting started with Time Pilot

Time Pilot records which project you work on, so you don't have to start and stop timers. At the end of the day you check your time and submit it.

## 1. Join your organization

1. Open the invitation email from Time Pilot and click **Accept invitation**.
2. Choose your password. That's your Time Pilot account.

No invitation? Ask your organization's admin.

## 2. Install the desktop app

Download it from **https://tracker.aasnatech.com/download/**.

- **Mac:** open the file and drag **Time Pilot** into **Applications**, then open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the developer: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.

## 3. Sign in

1. Click **Sign in**. Your browser opens: sign in there, then come back to the app.
2. If you belong to several organizations, choose the one to track time in.
3. Read the **Auto-tracking** screen, which explains what is captured, and click **I agree, turn on auto-tracking**.
4. **Mac only:** allow **Screen Recording** for Time Pilot when asked, then click **Quit & Reopen**.

That's it. The tray icon turns teal when a timer runs, and switching between projects happens by itself.

## Every day

- **Fix things:** tray → **Review today…** shows your blocks and screenshots. Change a project, edit times or split a block.
- **Private time:** tray → **Auto-tracking → Pause** stops tracking until you resume, or until midnight.
- **Pick by hand:** **⌘⇧T** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Shift+T** (Windows). Auto-tracking follows your choice for 30 minutes.
- **Submit:** in the evening a reminder asks you to check and **Submit** your day.

## Privacy

- **When:** a small grayscale screenshot every 5 minutes while tracking.
- **Where it's kept:** only on **your** computer, for 3 days. You can delete any of them in the review.
- **The AI:** when the window title isn't enough, the screenshot goes to the Time Pilot server, which asks the AI which project it is. The server doesn't keep it.
- **Never captured:** keystrokes, mouse activity, password managers, personal messengers, or anything while paused or locked.

## Problems?

Tray → **Open logs folder**, then send `time-pilot.log` to your admin. It contains no screenshots or window titles.
