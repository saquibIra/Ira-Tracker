Time Pilot

Your time, tracked to the right project. Automatically.

The Time Pilot desktop app recognises which project you work on, so you don't start and stop timers. At the end of the day you check your time and submit it.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

1. Join your organization

  1. Open the invitation email from Time Pilot and click Accept invitation.
  2. Choose your password. That's your Time Pilot account.

No invitation? Ask your organization's admin. Already have an account? Sign in.

2. Install the desktop app

  1. Mac: open the downloaded file and drag Time Pilot into Applications, then open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the developer: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  2. Windows: run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

3. Sign in

  1. Click Sign in. Your browser opens; sign in there and come back to the app.
  2. Read the auto-tracking screen and click I agree, turn on auto-tracking.
  3. Mac: allow Screen Recording for Time Pilot when asked, then click Quit & Reopen.

Every day: the menu bar or tray icon has Review today… to fix projects and times, Pause for private time, and an evening reminder to submit your day.

Privacy

While you track, a small grayscale screenshot is taken every few minutes. It stays on your computer for a few days so you can check your day, and you can delete any of them. Only when the window title is not enough is a screenshot sent to the server, to ask the AI which project it is; the server does not keep it. Keystrokes, mouse activity, password managers and personal messengers are never captured.
